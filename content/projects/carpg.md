Built at MakeNTU 2024. CaRPG is a proof-of-concept AR gunfight game for the cars of the future: passengers play by making hand gestures.

Gesture recognition and tracking run in real time with PyTorch and MediaPipe. [Watch the video](https://youtu.be/mxu7UHagzcg).

**Won 1st place in the AUO Enterprise Award at MakeNTU Hackathon 2024.**
