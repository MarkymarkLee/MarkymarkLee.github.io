Built at the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2023. Blue Vista is an educational mobile app that uses GPS and gamification to mix real-world exploration with learning about conservation and local ecology.

It's built with Flutter and Firebase.

**Won the Taiwan Space Agency Award at NASA Space Apps Challenge 2023.**
