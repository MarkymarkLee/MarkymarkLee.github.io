Built in 2 days with a team of 3 at LAUNCH-A-THON (Berkeley Haas), where UC Berkeley students work with founders from the UC LAUNCH Accelerator on a real problem.

BlueRobins wanted to reach more customers and keep the ones they had. We listened to what they asked for and built a web editor for their email newsletter. You build an issue from ready-made section cards (project highlight, mentor spotlight, testimonial, success story, quiz), and the app turns them into email-safe HTML inside BlueRobins' own header and footer, then sends it through SendGrid.

The app also runs an MCP server, so Claude can edit the same draft: add a mentor spotlight, rewrite the quiz, and the change shows up live in the editor. With Claude connected to their Google Drive as well, it can draft a whole newsletter from their own material in one shot.
