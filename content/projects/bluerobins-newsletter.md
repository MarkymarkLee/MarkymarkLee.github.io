Built in 2 days with a team of 3 at LAUNCH-A-THON (Berkeley Haas), where UC Berkeley students work with founders from the UC LAUNCH Accelerator on a real problem.

BlueRobins wanted to reach more customers and keep the ones they had. We listened to what they asked for, then built a newsletter creation tool in Next.js that is modular and matches their design.

We also built MCP servers that connect their Google Drive, so Claude can generate a whole newsletter in one shot from their own material.

**One of the winning teams at LAUNCH-A-THON 2026.**
