Course project for _Advanced Interactive Technologies_ (A+). In VR you play as a slime, and a pneumatic system lets you feel your size change in real time, which makes the experience much more immersive.

Soft-body physics in Unity drives ESP32-controlled air pumps that give the physical feedback. [Slides](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1gxudvnKEYP1wmsigZ6pb0rB4cd_14KiAmvFpk5qJz1M/edit?usp=sharing).
