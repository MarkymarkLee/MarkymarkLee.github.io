Course project for _Advanced Deep Learning_ (A+). E-MoTChi translates text into emoji by fine-tuning a pre-trained transformer language model. We validated it with human evaluation.

I also built a web app to visualize the model's predictions and evaluate how well it performs. [Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZJjq_77Nvso).
