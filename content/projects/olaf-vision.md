Course project for _Robot Perception and Learning_ (A+). We combined VLMs and imitation learning so people can guide a robot intuitively.

I built a React web app to collect human-corrected robot trajectories as training data. Along the way we found that VLMs struggle to understand 3D, and we found limits to what imitation learning could do here.
