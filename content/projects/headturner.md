Using a VR or MR headset while lying down is uncomfortable, and turning your head is hard when it rests on a pillow. HeadTurner is a device that assists shoulder and head actuation, so you can look around comfortably and see more of the scene from a reclined position.

I worked on it at the NTU HCI Lab (advisor: Prof. Mike Y. Chen) as a co-author. I helped design the device, implemented the EMG analysis, ran the user studies and wrote the related-work section of the paper.

[Read the paper](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3706598.3714214).

> Wu, En-Huei, et al. "HeadTurner: Enhancing Viewing Range and Comfort of using Virtual and Mixed-Reality Headsets while Lying Down via Assisted Shoulder and Head Actuation." _Proceedings of the 2025 CHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems_. 2025.
