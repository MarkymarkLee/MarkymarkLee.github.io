Built at MakeNTU 2023. Intellicoaster is a smart coaster that tracks your water consumption precisely and in real time.

An ESP32 microcontroller captures the data and Python stores it.

**Won 2nd place in the MediaTek Enterprise Award at MakeNTU Hackathon 2023.**
