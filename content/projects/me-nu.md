Built at the OpenHCI 2024 hackathon. Me_nu recommends dishes from a menu and lets you adjust your preferences as you go.

It's full stack: a Python backend that talks to ChatGPT, and a Vite web app where you tweak your preferences and see the recommendations update in real time.

**Won the Demonstration Award at OpenHCI 2024.**
